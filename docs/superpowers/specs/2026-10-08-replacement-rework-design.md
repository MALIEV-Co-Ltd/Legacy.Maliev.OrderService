## Ownership and invariants
An additive OrderService case refers to one customer and one or more affected original Order IDs. Original Order fields/history and invoice/quotation links never change through this module. Case IDs, production attempt IDs and shipment IDs are positive int identities scoped by the case; document/version identities are Guids. External Customer/Invoice/Quotation/Payment/Billing IDs remain scalar references; no cross-domain DbContexts/FKs. Use .NET 10, Npgsql, existing Order database, Redis and authenticated critical-write live permissions; no new infrastructure.

## Lifecycle
Reported -> Approved or Rejected. Approval records approver, reason, quantities, return Required/Waived, and explicit BlockProduction/BlockShipment choices. Required returns record received quantities and evidence; no-return has waiver reason. Production attempts allocate approved open demand per affected record. Attempt QA closes with accepted/rejected quantities summing to produced quantity, evidence and its own finished date. Failed QA releases unsuccessful demand for a further approved attempt; shipped accepted units cannot be produced again unless a recorded failed delivery is approved for another remedy attempt. Shipments allocate accepted unshipped quantities, with carrier/tracking/date and immutable delivery address/reference. Delivered vs failed delivery remain distinct; failures retain lineage. Closure requires approved quantities delivered or explicitly waived with audited reason, and no open production/shipment. Claims settle independently from remedy closure.

## Quantity protection
Use checked arithmetic, positive affected quantities <= original manufactured quantity, exact original customer ownership, immutable original snapshots and explicit affected IDs. Separate original sold/manufactured counts from replacement attempted/accepted/rejected/shipped/delivered counts. Atomic case version/idempotency ensures retries and concurrent commands cannot exceed open demand or double-count events. Historical repeated attempts do not increase original Remaining.

## Evidence and finance
Use shared private scanned FileService DocumentId/VersionId references and exact receipt identity/customer/original Order IDs. NDA scope inherited from original orders; expiry never makes data public. Producer unavailable => evidence-dependent critical command unavailable, never synthetic verified evidence. No tax-free rule; no automatic invoice/credit/payment/quote acceptance. Costs are operational facts with currency, original InvoiceId/QuotationId and optional acknowledged external accounting operation reference; refunds/credits/reimbursement postings remain Accounting-owned. Carrier claim stores reference/status/claimed/approved/received facts with evidence; no carrier provider action.

Existing legacy routes/DTOs remain stable.
